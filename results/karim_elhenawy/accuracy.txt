Settings:
  k = 5, voting rule = weighted (chosen by 5-fold cross-validation on training photos only, CV accuracy 0.934)
  ties: larger total num_good among the top k, then highest-ranked neighbour
  match score = num_good (SIFT, ratio test 0.75, RANSAC inliers), shared/matcher.py

Split:
  test = photos _1 to _5 of each landmark (30 photos)
  gallery = all other photos, _6 and up incl. _21 (91 photos)
  metric = accuracy = correct test predictions / all test predictions

Unknown-place detection (optional bonus)
Confidence: num_good of the best-matching training photo
Threshold: score < 52 -> 'unknown place' (chosen on training folds only)

  balanced accuracy:       0.857

Test photos (threshold fixed beforehand):

Unknown places are simulated by removing the photo's own landmark from the gallery.
